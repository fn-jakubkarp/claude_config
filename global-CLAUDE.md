# Writing rules
- Short negative guardrails are good: `NO em-dashes.` Keep them one line, no justification.
- Never wrap a rule in why/consequence/provenance. Drop `(confirmed <date>)` tags, `(MANDATORY)`, and one rule restated three ways.
- Delete rules guarding things already removed from the repo.
- Dropped means gone. No "rejected alternatives" lists, no names of tools we do not use.
- No defensive adverbs in headings: `How CVs are read`, not `How CVs are actually read`.
